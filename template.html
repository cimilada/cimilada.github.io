Method

  • Source. Daily series embedded in each station page on climseries.faoswalim.org: rain, temperature, humidity, pressure, solar radiation and wind. Aburin publishes hourly data from 2009; it is aggregated to days with at least 22 hourly rain readings.
  • Quality control. Rain outside 0–250 mm/day, temperature more than 8 °C from its 31-day median, humidity outside 3–100%, pressure more than 12 hPa from the station median, daily-mean radiation above 400 W/m² or below 20% of the clear-sky value, are rejected. Example rejects: 78,581 mm in a day at Bosaso, −20 °C at Qardho.
  • Reference ET. FAO-56 Penman-Monteith (Allen et al. 1998, eq. 6) on daily means. Elevation is inferred from station pressure because most station records list none. Wind is read as km/h at 2 m. Missing radiation is filled from the station's own monthly median and counted in the health table. Results match pyet.pm_fao56 within 0.03 mm/day.
  • Seasons. Jilaal Jan–Mar, Gu Apr–Jun, Hagaa Jul–Sep, Deyr Oct–Dec. Onset is the first day from 1 Mar (Gu) or 15 Sep (Deyr) with ≥ 20 mm over 3 days and no 10-day dry spell in the following 30 days. Classes use the UNEP aridity bounds 0.05, 0.20, 0.50 and 0.65 applied to the season.
  • Limits. Records are 2–5 years long, too short for a climatology, SPI or SPEI. Tmax and Tmin are not published, so saturation vapour pressure uses the daily mean, which slightly understates ET.

What exists on GitHub

Searches for faoswalim, climseries and SWALIM return no code that uses these stations. The nearest work uses gridded rainfall only, and the general tools are unaware of Somali seasons:

414★monocongo/climate_indices: SPI and SPEI. Needs about 30 years of data. 405★Ouranosinc/xclim: climate indicators on xarray, including dry spells. 180★woodcrafty/PyETo: FAO-56 building blocks. 165★pyet-org/pyet: reference evaporation. This page's ETo matches its FAO-56 result within 0.03 mm/day at every station. 2★mbsaid/eds_capstone_project: CHIRPS rainfall versus livestock in Somalia.

Why this project. Gridded products such as CHIRPS estimate rain. Only ground stations measure the radiation, wind and humidity that set crop and pasture water demand. This page combines both sides of the balance. It also shows which stations need a technician before Deyr.

Next steps. Run the scraper nightly. Blend CHIRPS to fill outages and give anomalies a 30-year base, which also makes SPEI possible. Alert SWALIM when a station goes silent or a gauge reads zero through rain elsewhere. Publish the cleaned daily set as open CSV.

Pipeline: scrape.py → process.py → build.py. Station coordinates and status as listed by SWALIM; boundaries from Natural Earth 1:50m.
